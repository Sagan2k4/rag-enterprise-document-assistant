# Compensation Guidelines

CONFIDENTIAL — HR only. Policy ID HR-022. Owner: People Operations.

## Salary Bands (Engineering, USD)

| Level | Title | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|-------|-------|---------|----------|---------|
| L3 | Software Engineer | $105,000 | $120,000 | $135,000 |
| L4 | Software Engineer II | $130,000 | $147,500 | $165,000 |
| L5 | Senior Software Engineer | $160,000 | $182,500 | $205,000 |
| L6 | Staff Software Engineer | $195,000 | $222,500 | $250,000 |

Offers above the band maximum require approval from the Chief People Officer.

## Annual Merit Cycle

The merit cycle runs every April, with increases effective from May 1. Merit increases are based on the performance rating:

- Exceeds expectations: 6% to 10%
- Meets expectations: 3% to 5%
- Partially meets expectations: 0% to 2%

The maximum combined merit and promotion increase in a single cycle is 12%.

## Promotions

Promotion packets are reviewed twice a year, in April and October. A promotion normally comes with an increase of 8% to 12%, and the new salary must be at least the minimum of the new band.

## Equity

New hires at L5 and above receive a stock option grant vesting over four years with a one-year cliff. Refresh grants are reviewed in the April cycle for employees rated "Exceeds expectations".
