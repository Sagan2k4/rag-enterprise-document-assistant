# Quarterly Close Procedures

Finance team only. Policy ID FIN-003. Owner: Corporate Controller.

## Close Timeline

The books are closed within 5 business days after quarter end (T+5):

- **T+1** — all sub-ledgers (AP, AR, payroll) locked; bank reconciliations started.
- **T+2** — accruals and prepaid amortisation booked.
- **T+3** — revenue recognition review and intercompany eliminations.
- **T+4** — flux analysis: any account moving more than 10% and $50,000 quarter over quarter needs a written explanation.
- **T+5** — Controller sign-off and reporting package sent to the CFO.

## Accrual Policy

Accrue any unbilled expense of $5,000 or more where the goods or services were received in the quarter. Smaller items are expensed when the invoice arrives.

## Revenue Recognition

Northwind follows ASC 606. Subscription revenue is recognised ratably over the contract term. Professional services revenue is recognised as services are delivered. Multi-year contracts paid upfront are recorded as deferred revenue.

## Journal Entry Approvals

Manual journal entries over $25,000 require review by a second accountant. Entries over $250,000 require Controller approval. Nobody may approve an entry they prepared.
