Travel and Expense Policy

Policy ID FIN-014. Owner: Finance. This page explains what Northwind Labs reimburses and how to claim it.

Booking Travel

All flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal (Navan) at least 14 days in advance where possible. Economy class is the standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from a vice president.

Hotels and Per Diem

The nightly hotel cap is $250 in most cities and $350 in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich and Tokyo).

The daily meal per diem is $75 for domestic travel and $100 for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client dinners.

Submitting Expenses

  • Submit every expense in Expensify within 30 days of the purchase date.
  • Attach an itemised receipt for every expense over $25.
  • Expenses submitted more than 90 days late will not be reimbursed.

Corporate Cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a corporate Amex card. Personal charges on the corporate card are not permitted and must be repaid within 10 days.
